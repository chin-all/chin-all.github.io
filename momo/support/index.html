遇到问题、或者它说了不合适的话

写邮件给我，通常几天内回复：

chiny.bf@gmail.com

App 里也可以直接发：设置 → 高级设置与支持 → 反馈。那样邮件会自动带上版本号和机型，不含任何对话内容。

不配置 API Key 能用吗？

能。它会眨眼、看你、随时间犯困或来精神，也会用自带的台词回应你。只是聊不深——想让它真正听懂你，需要接一个大模型服务。

怎么接大模型？

设置 → AI 与记忆 → AI 服务，选一家服务商（OpenAI、Anthropic，或者局域网里自己跑的 Ollama），填入你自己的 API Key。费用由你和服务商结算，momo 不收费、不抽成、不中转。

它为什么突然不说话了？

先看屏幕上有没有一行小字提示，比如 Key 无效或余额不足。其次，它夜里本来就比较安静。设置 → 高级设置与支持 → 自检 可以看到每一路传感器和模型连接的实时状态。

它会自己开口说话吗？

默认不会。设置 → 声音与互动 →「主动开口」打开后，你在的时候它大约一到两小时会主动找你说一句，夜里不说，你一直不理它它会自己变少。

「自然音色」是什么？

一个在设备上运行的语音模型，比系统音色自然得多。它不随 App 一起下载，需要时在 设置 → 声音与互动 里下载一次（约 200 MB，建议连 Wi-Fi）。

momo Support

A companion desk pet that lives in a spare iPhone or iPad.

Something's wrong, or it said something it shouldn't have

Email me. I usually reply within a few days:

chiny.bf@gmail.com

You can also send it from inside the app: Settings → Advanced & Support → Feedback. That email pre-fills the version and device model, with no conversation content.

Does it work without an API key?

Yes. It blinks, watches you, gets sleepy or perks up over the day, and answers with its own built-in lines. It just can't go deep. To have it really understand you, connect a language model.

How do I connect a language model?

Settings → AI & Memory → AI providers. Pick a provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or an Ollama server on your own network) and enter your own API key. You pay the provider directly; momo charges nothing, takes no cut, and never proxies your conversations.

Why did it stop talking?

First check for a small line of text on screen, such as an invalid key or no credit left. It's also naturally quieter at night. Settings → Advanced & Support → Self-check shows the live status of every sensor and the model connection.

Will it start talking on its own?

Not by default. Turn on Settings → Voice & Interaction → "Speak on its own" and it will occasionally start a conversation, roughly once every one to two hours while you're around, never at night, and less often if you keep ignoring it.

What is the "natural voice"?

An on-device speech model that sounds far more natural than the system voice. It isn't bundled with the app; download it once from Settings → Voice & Interaction when you want it (about 200 MB, Wi-Fi recommended).
