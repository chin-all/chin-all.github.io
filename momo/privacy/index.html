momo 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 24 日

1. 概述

momo 是一只运行在 iPhone / iPad 上的陪伴型 AI 桌宠。本隐私政策说明我们如何处理你在使用 momo 时的数据。

简言之：momo 不运营后端。你的数据默认在你的设备上。大模型对话是可选的，需要你自己配置和支付；那些数据直接发往你选择的服务商，不经过 momo。

2. App 会用到哪些数据

下面这些数据只在你的设备上被使用，开发者收不到其中任何一项。

2.1 摄像头（可选）

用途：判断你在不在，以及测量环境光线。

  • 摄像头画面全程在你的设备上处理，从不录制、不保存、不上传
  • 不做人脸识别或身份识别
  • 权限可以跳过，跳过后 App 照常工作

2.2 麦克风（可选）

用途：用于语音对话，以及可选的环境音感知（只测响度，不测内容）。

  • 语音识别优先在你的设备上进行
  • 音频不被录制保存，不离开设备
  • 权限可以跳过

2.3 位置（可选，默认关闭）

用途：判断你大概在"家/公司/别处"这类地点类型，以及获取天气信息。

  • 坐标在离开设备前会被粗化到约 1 公里精度
  • App 只保存地点类型标签和粗化后的锚点坐标，不保存轨迹、不做后台跟踪
  • 权限可以跳过

2.4 对话内容（可选）

用途：与第三方大模型服务进行对话。

  • 用户必须自己配置第三方大模型服务（如 OpenAI、Anthropic、局域网 Ollama 等）并填入自己的 API Key
  • 对话内容直接从你的设备发往你选择的那个服务商，momo 不做中转、看不到内容
  • 这些数据如何被处理，完全取决于你选择的服务商，请查阅该服务商的隐私政策
  • 完全不配置 API Key 也能使用 momo，此时它只用本地台词回应，不会发出任何与对话有关的网络请求

2.5 天气数据

如果你开启了位置权限，momo 会使用粗化后的坐标向 Open-Meteo（开放式天气服务）请求天气数据，不发送任何身份信息。

2.6 API Key

你配置的 API Key 存储在 iOS 系统 Keychain 里，不离开你的设备。

2.7 自然音色模型（可选）

如果你在设置里选择下载「自然音色」，模型文件会从 Apple 的服务器下载（App Store 的按需资源机制），与下载 App 本身相同，不涉及任何个人信息。之后语音合成全程在设备上完成。

3. 我们不收集什么

  • 不收集、不传输、不存储任何用户数据到开发者控制的任何服务器
  • 没有第三方分析 SDK、没有广告、没有崩溃上报
  • 不生成任何用户标识符
  • 不做用户跟踪或行为分析

4. 宠物状态与记忆

你的宠物状态、记忆、相处天数等数据全部保存在你设备的 App 沙箱里，不离开你的设备。这些数据随 App 卸载而删除。你也可以在 App 设置中查看和清除记忆。

5. 儿童

momo 不针对 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童（指 13 岁以下）的任何信息。由于 momo 不向开发者传输任何数据，我们也无从持有任何儿童的信息。

6. 数据删除

删除 momo App 即删除全部本地数据。对于通过 API Key 发送给第三方服务商的对话数据，其删除遵循该服务商的隐私政策。

7. 你的权利

由于 momo 不运营后端，不存储你的任何数据副本，因此：

  • 你可以随时卸载 App 删除全部本地数据
  • 你完全掌控哪些权限（摄像头、麦克风、位置）被授予给 momo
  • 你完全掌控是否配置和使用大模型服务
  • 所有数据都在你的设备上，没有云端副本

8. 第三方服务

momo 可能连接以下第三方服务，具体取决于你的配置和选择：

  • 大模型服务：OpenAI、Anthropic、本地 Ollama 等（用户选择和付费）
  • 天气服务：Open-Meteo（开源天气服务，无需认证）

请查阅这些服务商的隐私政策了解它们如何处理你的数据。

9. 开发者

momo 由个人开发者维护，没有任何服务器基础设施。

10. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。重大变更会在此页面标注新的生效日期。继续使用 momo 即表示接受更新后的政策。

11. 联系方式

如有关于本隐私政策的问题，请联系开发者：

chiny.bf@gmail.com

最后一句话：momo 的设计原则是克制。我们不收集超过必要的数据，不与任何人分享你的信息，不在你的设备之外维护你的任何副本。你的隐私和你的数据完全在你手中。

momo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

1. Overview

momo is a companion AI desk pet for iPhone and iPad. This privacy policy explains how we handle data when you use momo.

In short: momo has no backend. Your data stays on your device by default. Large language model conversations are optional and require you to configure and pay for a third-party service; that data goes directly to the provider you choose, not through momo.

2. What Data the App Uses

Everything below is used on your device only. None of it ever reaches the developer.

2.1 Camera (Optional)

Purpose: To determine if you are present and to measure ambient light.

  • Camera frames are processed entirely on your device and are never recorded, saved, or uploaded
  • No facial recognition or identity identification is performed
  • Permission can be skipped; momo works normally without it

2.2 Microphone (Optional)

Purpose: For voice conversations and optional ambient sound sensing (which measures loudness only, not content).

  • Speech recognition is prioritized on your device
  • Audio is never recorded or saved and stays on your device
  • Permission can be skipped

2.3 Location (Optional, Disabled by Default)

Purpose: To determine your general location type (home/work/elsewhere) and to fetch weather information.

  • Coordinates are coarsened to approximately 1 km precision before leaving your device
  • momo stores only location type labels and coarsened anchor coordinates, not trajectories or background tracking
  • Permission can be skipped

2.4 Conversation Content (Optional)

Purpose: To communicate with third-party large language model services.

  • You must configure a third-party LLM service (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, local Ollama) and provide your own API key
  • Conversation content is sent directly from your device to your chosen provider; momo does not relay or access this data
  • How this data is handled depends entirely on the provider you choose; please review their privacy policy
  • momo works without any API key configured; in that case it responds with built-in local lines and makes no conversation-related network requests

2.5 Weather Data

If you enable location permission, momo requests weather data from Open-Meteo (an open weather service) using your coarsened coordinates and sends no identifying information.

2.6 API Keys

Any API keys you configure are stored in the iOS system Keychain and never leave your device.

2.7 Natural Voice Model (optional)

If you choose to download the "natural voice" in Settings, the model files are downloaded from Apple's servers through the App Store's on-demand resources mechanism, the same way the app itself is delivered. No personal information is involved, and speech synthesis then runs entirely on your device.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any user data on servers controlled by the developer
  • No third-party analytics SDKs, advertising, or crash reporting
  • No user identifiers are generated
  • No user tracking or behavioral analysis

4. Pet State and Memories

Your pet's state, memories, days together, and related data are stored entirely in your device's App sandbox and never leave your device. This data is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also view and clear memories in the app's settings.

5. Children

momo is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Because momo transmits no data to the developer, we hold no information about anyone, children included.

6. Data Deletion

Uninstalling momo deletes all local data. For conversation data sent to third-party providers via API key, deletion is governed by that provider's privacy policy.

7. Your Rights

Because momo has no backend and does not store any copies of your data elsewhere, you have direct control:

  • You can uninstall the app at any time to delete all local data
  • You fully control which permissions (camera, microphone, location) momo receives
  • You fully control whether to configure and use an LLM service
  • All data exists on your device only; there is no cloud copy

8. Third-Party Services

momo may connect to the following third-party services depending on your configuration and choices:

  • Large Language Models: OpenAI, Anthropic, local Ollama, etc. (you choose and pay)
  • Weather Service: Open-Meteo (open-source weather service, no authentication required)

Please review the privacy policies of these services to understand how they handle your data.

9. Developer

momo is maintained by an independent developer with no server infrastructure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be marked with a new effective date on this page. Continued use of momo constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer:

chiny.bf@gmail.com

Final note: momo is designed with restraint. We collect only what is necessary, share nothing with anyone, and maintain no copies of your data outside your device. Your privacy and your data are entirely in your control.
